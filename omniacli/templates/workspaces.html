Workspaces

A workspace is a customer's closed part of this machine: its servers, and its users, each with its role there (user, developer, admin, as on the Users page). A user of role workspace (Users page) sees only the servers of its workspaces, none of this machine's pages, consoles, links and configuration; the admins of a workspace manage its users. A server is in one workspace at most. Saved in {{ workspaces_file }}.
