Links

Other OmniaCLI instances this one operates: their servers are listed on the Dashboard and their pages open here, under /links/<name>/. The other instance creates the token on its Users page and decides its role; here each user acts there with at most their own role. Saved in {{ links_file }}, readable by its owner only: it holds the tokens.

A customer's server that shows only SSH: give the link an SSH server.
