Tunnels

A tunnel opens a port of this machine ({{ options.tunnel_bind }}, ports {{ options.tunnel_ports }}) that reaches the web interface of an instance of a linked OmniaCLI, through the link: for when this OmniaCLI reaches it and your browser does not. {% if options.tunnel_bind.startswith("127.") or options.tunnel_bind == "::1" %}Only a browser on this machine reaches it.{% endif %} It closes when you close it, when OmniaCLI stops, or after {{ options.tunnel_idle_hours }} h without traffic. Anyone who reaches the port reaches the instance's own login: the tunnel adds no login of its own. The browser keeps a site's cookies by address, not by port: two tunnels open at once share Odoo's login.
