Pullers

This instance relays between an OmniaCLI that cannot be reached (the producer) and the OmniaCLI that operate it (the consumers): both link out to here, with an incoming link of the Users page (the role user is enough). The producer takes the consumers' commands and leaves its reports and backups here; with a key, which only the two ends hold, this instance cannot read nor change them. A command not taken by the producer in 10 minutes expires; a file goes once a consumer has it whole, and every file and report after the hours kept. Saved in {{ pullers_file }}.

Published here

Instances of this OmniaCLI published as the producer of a puller of a relay, reached through a link of the Links page: this instance asks the relay for commands and sends it its reports (state, disks, load, the progress of its jobs) and its backups. Every command is off until you enable it; one sent outside its hours (HH:MM-HH:MM by this machine's clock, empty: always) is refused at once. With a key, give the same key to the consumers: without one, whoever controls the relay can read what passes and send the commands enabled.
