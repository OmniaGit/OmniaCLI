OmniaDisk.render(box, disk, opts) fills box with one disk: disk: {label, title, used, free, total, reserved, forecast, href, note} used, free, total: bytes; free defaults to total - used (a disk of the machine passes its own: ext4 keeps 5% for root) reserved: bytes promised on it and not used yet (a real disk under workspaces), shown as a lighter part of the bar after the used one forecast: diskhistory.forecast ({full, per_day, days, reason}), the estimated consumption; none: not recorded yet href: its history (the Disks page) note: one more line under the bar opts.compact: one line (a table cell, a machine's row): the bar, the percent used and, when near, the day it is full; the rest in its title; with href, a link to it OmniaDisk.level(disk): "ok", "warn" or "error", the same thresholds as disks.py (free under 15% and 5%), or a full day within 90 and 30 days. OmniaDisk.size(bytes): the size as text. OmniaDisk.workspace(box, name, load): a workspace's disk from the answer of /api/workspaces//load (its limit, or its image's size), or why not. OmniaDisk.realDisk(space, storage): the real disk under a storage, from /api/workspaces/space: what the workspaces were promised there and do not use yet is the room of a new one ("") reserved. #}
